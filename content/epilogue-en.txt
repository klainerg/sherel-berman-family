The story of the Sherel and Berman families is the story of two Jewish families who lived in the same historical region but followed different paths during the Second World War.

The Sherel family was connected to the town of Orinin and a Jewish community that was almost destroyed. Grisha and his relatives survived the war and returned to rebuild their lives.

The Berman family lost its father, Israel, during the war. Eva and her children survived a lengthy evacuation journey but lost little Galochka along the way. Other relatives were murdered under Nazi occupation.

In 1949, Grisha and Dasha married, and two family stories became one.

With the births of Igor and Nelia, the next generation of the Sherel family continued. Nelia's marriage to Fima Kleiner brought the stories of three families — Sherel, Berman and Kleiner — together on a shared family path.

In 1991, when Dasha immigrated to Israel with Nelia, Fima and Gena, a new chapter began in the family story, which had started generations earlier in the Jewish towns of Podolia.

The remaining documents, preserved photographs and memories passed down from generation to generation allow us today to retrace the family's journey and the connection between generations.
