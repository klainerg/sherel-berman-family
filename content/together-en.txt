## Orinin, 28 December 1949

After the war, life gradually returned to its course, but the world to which the survivors returned was no longer the one they had known.

Grigory Sherel, born in 1924, survived the war and lived in the Orinin area.

Dasha Berman, born in 1926, survived the flight from Lviv and the years of evacuation with her mother and brother.

On 28 December 1949, Grigory Iosifovich Sherel and Dasha Israelovna Berman married in Orinin, in the Kamianets-Podilskyi district.

Grigory was about 25 years old, and Dasha was 23.

On that day, two family paths came together: the Sherel family from Orinin and the Berman family, who had endured flight and evacuation during the war years.

## Starting a new family

Grisha and Dasha established their home in the post-war Soviet Union.

[[תמונה: dasha-at-work.png | Dasha at work]]

The couple had two children: Igor (Игорь), the elder, and Nelia (Неля), who was born in 1957. Igor and Nelia were Iosif’s grandchildren and the great-grandchildren of Naum, Zakharia’s son.

[[תמונה: nelia-child.png | Nelia Kleiner, née Sherel, as a child]]

The children grew up while the Soviet Union was trying to recover from the devastation left by the war, and memories of the difficult years were still preserved in the family.

Grisha Sherel died in 1974 in Orinin, Ukraine, when Nelia was about 16 years old.

After his death, Dasha continued her life with her children, Igor and Nelia.

## The next generation — from Kamianets-Podilskyi to Chernivtsi

Over the years, Nelia also started a family of her own. She married Fima Kleiner — the son of Shika Kleiner and Ania Beznos — from the Kleiner family, whose origins were in Sokyriany.

Nelia and Fima lived in Chernivtsi (Черновцы), where they established their family.

Another family connection was thus formed, this time between the descendants of the Sherel and Berman families and the Kleiner family.

Igor, Nelia's older brother, lived in Kamianets-Podilskyi with his mother Dasha, while Nelia and Fima built their lives in Chernivtsi.

[[תמונה: igor-and-gena.png | Igor Sherel and little Gena Kleiner]]

In 1990, Igor Sherel died.

A year later, in 1991, Dasha immigrated to Israel with her daughter Nelia, Nelia's husband Fima Kleiner, and their son Gena (Гена).

Immigration to Israel marked a new chapter in the family's history. After generations of life in Podolia and the Soviet Union, this branch of the family continued its journey in a new country.
