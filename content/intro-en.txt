The roots of the Sherel and Berman families lie in Podolia, Ukraine, a region that was home to Jewish communities for generations. At the beginning of the twentieth century, both families lived in the Soviet Union, but their lives followed different paths.

The Sherel family was connected to the town of Orinin and to Kamianets-Podilskyi, while the Berman family moved between cities because of the father's work.

The Second World War changed the lives of both families. Family members faced the German occupation, flight, loss and separation, and after the war they tried to rebuild their lives.

In 1949, the two family stories came together when Grigory (Grisha) Sherel and Dasha Berman married in Orinin.
